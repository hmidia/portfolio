case study

Planally

Planally is a workflow platform that helps teams manage tasks, processes, and collaboration in one place. It is designed for complex, multi-step workflows across different teams and departments. The challenge was to reduce fragmentation and make structured processes easier to understand and manage in a single system. I worked on the end-to-end UI/UX of the web platform, focusing on simplifying complexity while keeping the system scalable.

role

UI/UX Designer

I led the design of end-to-end user flows while shaping reusable components to keep the experience consistent and scalable.

problem

Teams managing workflows often deal with

  • Too many disconnected tools
  • Lack of visibility across tasks and progress
  • Complex processes that are hard to follow and maintain

Designed the dashboard experience

I designed the main dashboard to give users a clear overview of ongoing tasks, workflow status, and progress across different projects. The focus was on making key information easy to scan and act on.

Designed gated workflow structure

I worked on structuring gated workflows, where each step depends on the completion of the previous one. I designed the UI and flow logic to make these transitions clear, controlled, and easy to follow.

outcome

The final experience made complex workflows easier to manage by giving users a clear dashboard view and structured gated processes. Users could better understand progress across different stages, while the consistent UI system helped reduce confusion and improved overall clarity in managing work.
