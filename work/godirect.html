case study

Takaful IKHLAS
Go Direct

Go Direct lets people buy and renew takaful online, without an agent. It covers motor, travel, personal accident and fire/home, and each one works a little differently. My job was to turn all of that into one purchase journey people could trust, from quotation to payment.

role

UI/UX Designer

I designed the end-to-end flows and the reusable components behind them, and used data to find where people got stuck.

problem

Buying takaful is usually

  • Complicated
  • Agent-dependent
  • Hard to understand

Designed the full purchase journey

One clear path from quotation to payment, where every step tells you what comes next. No agent needed to get through it.

Made pricing and coverage easier to understand

The premium updates as you choose, and coverage is broken down plainly, so people can compare options and know what they’re paying for before they commit.

Unified multiple products into one system

Every product now follows the same structure, so once you’ve bought one, the rest feel familiar.

outcome

One purchase flow now serves every product, and people can see the full price and coverage before they pay.
