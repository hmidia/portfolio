case study

Betacard
Mobile App

Betacard is a mobile app that allows users to create, share, and manage digital business cards. It replaces physical cards with a contactless system where users can instantly share details through QR, links, or nearby sharing. It also allows users to scan physical cards and store them digitally for easier contact management. I worked on the mobile UX, focusing on making networking feel fast, simple, and natural in real-life situations.

role

UI/UX Designer

I designed end-to-end user flows while shaping reusable components to keep the experience simple and consistent.

problem

Traditional business cards are

  • Static and hard to update
  • Not flexible for personalization
  • Limited in how they represent personal or brand identity

Designed the customization experience

A major part of my work was designing how users personalize their cards. I focused on:

  • Layout structure of customization tools
  • Selection of effects and styles
  • How users preview changes in real time

The goal was to make customization feel flexible but not overwhelming.

Designed card templates

I created a set of reusable templates that users can choose from as a starting point, making it easier for them to create professional-looking cards quickly without starting from scratch.

outcome

The final experience made it easier for users to create personalized digital business cards without complexity. By combining templates with a flexible customization system, users could quickly build cards that reflect their identity while still having control over design details. The flow-based structure ensured the experience remained simple, consistent, and easy to navigate.
